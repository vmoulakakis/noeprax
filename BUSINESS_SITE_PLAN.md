# NOEPRAX — Automated AI Services Business & Website Plan

## 1. Strategic thesis

NOEPRAX should not launch as a generic "AI consultancy agency". The scalable model is a productized AI-services platform for SMEs and independent professionals.

Core promise:

> Intelligence into Action — practical AI systems that save time, capture revenue, and improve operations.

Primary commercial model:

1. Visitor selects profession / business type.
2. NOEPRAX diagnoses the highest-value workflow gaps.
3. The site recommends a fixed-scope package.
4. Customer purchases or books a qualified implementation call.
5. Automated onboarding collects website, brand, FAQs, calendar/CRM/email requirements and consent.
6. An orchestration layer provisions the selected AI services from reusable templates.
7. Automated QA checks functionality, privacy, integrations and hallucination risk.
8. Human approval remains required for sensitive/high-risk workflows.
9. Customer receives a dashboard and recurring outcome reports.
10. Usage, performance and business value drive automated upgrade recommendations.

Target automation level: 80–90% of sales, onboarding, provisioning, reporting and routine optimization. Do not promise 100% autonomous delivery for legal, medical, financial, security-sensitive or business-critical workflows.

## 2. Target market

### Initial wedge

Start with Greece / Cyprus / English-speaking EU businesses, then expand to international English-language markets.

Best early customer profiles:

- 1–20 person businesses with recurring inbound enquiries
- appointment-based businesses
- local professional services
- businesses losing leads outside working hours
- businesses with repetitive email / phone / admin work
- businesses that already use Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, calendars, CRMs or ecommerce platforms
- founders and freelancers who need sales/admin leverage without hiring additional staff

### Expansion

After repeatable delivery and retention are proven, localize by country and profession rather than creating one generic global site.

Example route structure:

- /gr/dentists
- /gr/lawyers
- /gr/accountants
- /uk/estate-agents
- /us/real-estate-agents
- /au/trades

Each vertical page should have its own pains, workflows, ROI model, package recommendation, demo and compliance notes.

## 10. Pricing strategy

Do not compete as the cheapest AI agency.

Use three levels:

### Launch

€99–299/month

Single high-value automation. Designed for solo professionals and microbusinesses.

### Growth

€349–799/month

Multiple connected automations and dashboard/reporting. Main target for 3–20 employee businesses.

### Business OS

€999–2,500+/month

Integrated front desk + sales + operations + analytics + optimization.

Custom integrations should carry implementation fees. Avoid unlimited AI usage. Define fair-use or metered usage for voice, messaging and expensive agentic workflows.

Pricing should be localized by market and value, not converted mechanically from EUR.

## 11. Global business strategy

NOEPRAX can become global, but not by selling generic "AI consulting worldwide".

The defensible path is:

1. Productize outcomes.
2. Build reusable vertical templates.
3. Make onboarding self-serve where possible.
4. Centralize model/tool/integration infrastructure.
5. Localize acquisition pages, pricing, language and compliance.
6. Keep high-risk actions governed.
7. Measure ROI at customer level.
8. Turn repeated implementation patterns into software.

Long-term transition:

Agency → Productized Service → Managed AI Platform → Vertical AI Operating System.

This progression improves gross margin and makes international scaling possible.

## 15. Non-negotiables

- Sell outcomes, not AI buzzwords.
- Do not promise fully autonomous operation where human accountability is required.
- No fabricated testimonials, clients, scarcity or ROI statistics.
- Every customer-facing AI system needs logging, evaluation, escalation and rollback.
- Build one reusable platform, not bespoke codebases for every profession.
- Verticalize the customer experience; centralize the technical core.
- Optimize for recurring revenue and measurable customer value.

## Final business definition

NOEPRAX is not an AI agency with a catalogue of projects.

> NOEPRAX is a managed AI-services platform that gives small businesses and independent professionals ready-to-deploy AI workers for customer service, sales, administration and decision support — configured for their profession, integrated with their tools, governed safely, and priced as recurring services.
